Personal Video Uploader

A desktop publishing tool for uploading finished videos to the owner’s YouTube channel.

This page describes the personal uploader connected to the Google OAuth app named speech. The uploader runs on the owner’s computer and uses the official YouTube Data API.

What it does

  • Checks the name and ID of the channel selected during Google authorization.
  • Uploads a chosen video, title, description and publication settings to that channel.
  • Keeps local upload receipts to help prevent duplicate uploads.

Your channel, your authorization

Google authorization requests permission to upload videos and view YouTube account information. The tool checks the authorized channel before each upload. The owner chooses what to upload and its visibility; this installation defaults to public.

There is no public sign-up or upload form on this information page. Access to a channel requires that channel owner’s Google authorization. You can revoke access at any time in Google Account connections.

Before connecting
